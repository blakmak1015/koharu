- Write numbers with Western digits (0-9), exactly as the source writes them. Do not convert them to the target language's own numeral system.

- Never write a hedged Thai politeness particle. "ครับ/ค่ะ", "ค่ะ/ครับ" and "คะ/ครับ" are forbidden. Choose exactly one particle, or write none.
- Choose from the speaker's own Japanese, including the other segments on this page when the same character speaks more than once.
  Male speaker: 僕 ぼく 俺 おれ 自分 わし 拙者 だぜ だぞ だな かい かね しろ ぞ ぜ
  Female speaker: あたし わたくし かしら だわ ですわ のよ わよ わね くださいまし ますわ ことよ
- 〜でござる and 〜でございます mark a retainer or a servant. Follow this series' character sheet when one is given below; with no sheet, treat the speaker as male and use ครับ.
- When any of those markers is present you must choose a particle. Only a segment that carries no marker at all may be written as polite Thai with no gendered particle.
